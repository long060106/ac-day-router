The three rules this runs on

Rule 1 — Zone days

One zone per day. Emergencies break the rule; nothing else does.

Miami to Boca Raton is 45–50 miles and 50–70 minutes, 90 in traffic. Two round trips up the corridor in one day is 4 wasted hours. Maintenance, quotes, drain clogs and reschedules can all wait two days — so they get batched by zone.

Rule 2 — Hot places early

Attic and roof before the cutoff. Indoor units in the afternoon.

A South Florida attic passes 130–140°F within minutes of a mid-90s afternoon. 6–9a is the window for real attic work; 9–10:30a is short diagnostics only; 11a–5p should be emergencies only. So the day sorts hot-first, and the app moves any attic job that lands past the cutoff to the next morning instead of cooking a tech.

Rule 3 — Honest time blocks

Symptom sets the base. Access and tonnage stretch it.

Booking every call as "one hour" is what makes the day collapse by 2pm. Base minutes are one tech, small system, easy access; the multipliers do the rest. Two techs cut it to 65%, not 50% — someone still has to talk to the customer and write it up.
